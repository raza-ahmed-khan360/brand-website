# Tangible | Full-Service Digital Growth & Execution Agency

A handcrafted, high-converting agency landing page offering services similar to **GoExecution**, built with a **clean, light-theme Claymorphism design system**.

Designed to avoid AI-generated tropes (zero cheap linear gradients, zero unnatural glints or flares, zero buzzword soup). Everything uses solid, tactile clay surfaces, realistic molded depth, and grounded commercial copy.

---

## 🎨 Design System Highlights

- **Aesthetic**: Clean, light-theme Claymorphism.
- **Color Discipline**:
  - Dominant Neutral: Chalk / light porcelain ceramic ground (`#EEF2F7`).
  - Secondary: Molded matte ceramic cards (`#FFFFFF`) with multi-layered soft outer shadows and inset bevels (`#E5ECF4`).
  - Single Accent: Warm Terracotta Clay (`#E2583E`) with deep slate typography (`#0F172A`).
  - Strict Ban: **No gradients**, **no neon/glint highlights**, **zero em-dashes or en-dashes**.
- **Tactile Micro-Interactions**:
  - 5-State control matrix on buttons and inputs (Hover lift, Active scale compression, Focus-visible rings).
  - Floating sculpted clay cards.
  - Inset wells for chips, badges, and progress counters.

7. **Storytelling Section**: "The Strategy-Execution Chasm" contrasting sluggish traditional agencies with Tangible's agile 4-stage sprint protocol.
8. **Pricing Section**: Modular pricing with interactive Monthly Retainer Squads vs Fixed Milestone Sprints switcher.
9. **About Section**: "The Operators Behind The Code", core operating principles, global sprint pods (Dallas HQ, London, Singapore), and track record metrics.
10. **Testimonials Section**: Verified founder quotes with quantifiable outcome badges.
11. **FAQs Section**: Clay accordion addressing common onboarding, IP ownership, and pricing questions.
12. **Main CTA Section**: High-conversion terminal with diagnostic inquiry form and direct Dallas HQ contact details.
13. **Footer**: Directory, vertical links, live Central Time clock, and system operational indicator.
14. **Interactive Audit Modal**: Standalone growth diagnostic modal triggered from anywhere on the page with toast notifications.
