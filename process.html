04 / OUR METHODOLOGY

Simple on purpose.
Thorough by design.

We eliminated unnecessary account managers, endless status meetings, and bloated bureaucracy. Our 4-phase agile delivery sprint ensures you receive high-velocity craftsmanship, verified milestones, and transparent code handoff.

FRAMEWORK: 04 SPRINT PHASES COLLABORATION: ASYNC & DIRECT VELOCITY: CONTINUOUS STAGING REVIEWS

THE SPRINT

Four focused phases from
concept to deployment.

Every phase delivers concrete review artifacts so you stay fully informed at every turn.

PHASE / 01 DISCOVERY & ARCHITECTURE

01. Understand & Architect

We start by interrogating your business model, customer buying journeys, competitor benchmarks, and conversion objectives.

Key Milestones & Artifacts
  • Comprehensive project brief and target audience persona review
  • Sitemap tree and user flow wireframes
  • Technical stack selection (WordPress, PHP, Next.js, or headless)
  • Content requirements and asset inventory checklist
  • Milestone sprint roadmap and delivery timeline signoff
DURATION: DAYS 1 TO 5 OUTPUT: ARCHITECTURE BLUEPRINT
PHASE / 02 CREATIVE DIRECTION

02. Create & Prototype

Initial concepts evolve into high-fidelity design systems. We establish typography scales, color palettes, and responsive screen prototypes.

Key Milestones & Artifacts
  • High-fidelity Figma prototypes for mobile and desktop
  • Brand design tokens: serif and sans typography, color palette
  • Interactive UI micro-interactions and button hover states
  • Asynchronous Loom video walkthrough highlighting design choices
  • Structured revision feedback loop and client approval
DURATION: DAYS 6 TO 12 OUTPUT: APPROVED FIGMA PROTOTYPE
PHASE / 03 PRODUCTION DEVELOPMENT

03. Engineer & Build

Approved designs are translated into production code. We build with clean semantic HTML, modular CSS, lightweight JavaScript, and responsive layouts.

Key Milestones & Artifacts
  • Semantic markup adhering strictly to accessibility standards
  • CSS architecture optimized for sub-second Core Web Vitals
  • Dynamic pricing catalogs and interactive portfolio filter logic
  • Contact form integrations with anti-spam honeypot and email webhooks
  • Live staging URL for hands-on client testing
DURATION: DAYS 13 TO 19 OUTPUT: WORKING STAGING SITE
PHASE / 04 QA & DEPLOYMENT

04. Refine & Launch

Rigorous cross-browser testing, device compatibility verification, SEO metadata validation, and seamless production deployment.

Key Milestones & Artifacts
  • Cross-browser testing across Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox
  • Mobile breakpoint verification across iOS and Android devices
  • Search Console verification, XML sitemap submission, and 301 redirects
  • Domain DNS pointing and SSL security certificate deployment
  • 100% intellectual property, source code, and asset handoff
DURATION: DAYS 20 TO 24 OUTPUT: LIVE PRODUCTION LAUNCH
